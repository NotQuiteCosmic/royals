Moves

    Reading the board

    • R royal · S spy · a number is that many pawns
    • D is the dragon: it flies over things, cannot be captured, and cannot win
    • A dim strip under a stack is prisoners — pieces of the other colour held there
    • A gold ring marks a move that wraps around the edge of the board
    • A stack holding a spy can break: select it, press BREAK, then pick a heading to scatter along
    • Shoving a square that holds your people can push them along or free them — click it and the board asks which
